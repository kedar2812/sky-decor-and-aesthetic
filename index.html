Dear friend,

I started Sky Decor in 2019 because I kept walking into celebrations that looked the same. Same drapes, same flowers, same lights, whatever the family or the story.

Six years on we are forty people: designers, florists, carpenters, electricians and coordinators, working out of one workshop on the edge of the city. Every event starts as a drawing on my desk and ends with our crew carrying the last crate out at dawn.

We take on a limited number of celebrations each season, so the person who sketches your mandap is the person who lights it on the night.

With warmth,

Sumit

Founder & creative director

Event production

We conceive the whole environment and build it ourselves: stages and mandaps, floral installations, lighting, furniture and fabrication. Large pieces are assembled at full size in our workshop before they travel, so heights, colours and sightlines are settled weeks ahead.

Concept and mood boards, floral and botanical work, set fabrication, lighting design, furniture and tablescapes.

Event management

One team runs the day so your family can be guests at it. A lead coordinator is yours from booking day, and on site from the first load-in to the last car leaving. Vendors, timings, guest movement and every cue from baraat to bidaai.

Planning and budgets, vendor management, guest hospitality, run-of-show, on-site crew.

Planning something smaller at home? We style pujas, anniversaries and dinners for up to sixty guests too. Ask about intimate styling

Monsoon Garden
